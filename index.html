About

I am a researcher in bioinformatics and computational biology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), working with Prof. Kang Ning's group. My research focuses on developing deep learning and large-scale pretrained (foundation) models for microbiome data analysis — including microbial community dynamics, cancer diagnosis and prognosis from microbiome profiles, biosynthetic gene cluster mining, and antimicrobial peptide discovery.
