I train foundation models that take a community as sequence, abundance, and context, so a representation learned in one cohort can be used in another, and read for what it actually relied on.

MGM2

MGM2 as a Multimodal Foundation Model for Microbial Community States and Responses

1.8 million
Microbiomes in pretraining.
0.91
Mean macro-AUROC on later MGnify projects absent from pretraining.
68%
Of observed high-change taxa recovered in FMT outcome prediction.

Quoted from the MGM2 v2 abstract, bioRxiv, posted 30 September 2026. This page does not recompute them.

MGM2 builds a community representation from three things at once: a DNA language-model embedding of each microbe’s sequence, that microbe’s abundance, and a description of the sample. The revised preprint follows those representations through transplantation, drugs, and recovery, and uses sparse features to show which signals the model is using.

About

I started the direct-track doctorate at HUST in 2023, after a bachelor’s degree at the same university, and I work in Prof. Kang Ning’s group.

The subject throughout is the microbial community. The tools change: foundation models, dynamics, biosynthetic gene clusters, antimicrobial peptides, and agents that can run a metagenomic analysis and leave a record of what they did.
