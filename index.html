About

I am a researcher in bioinformatics and computational biology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), working in Prof. Kang Ning's group. My research focuses on developing deep learning and large-scale pretrained foundation models for microbiome analysis — including microbial community dynamics, cancer diagnosis and prognosis from microbiome profiles, biosynthetic gene cluster mining, and antimicrobial peptide discovery.

Research Directions

Microbiome Foundation Models

Large-scale pretrained and generative language models (MGM) for interpretable, general-purpose microbiome data analysis across diverse contexts.

Cancer Microbiome Diagnostics

AI-driven diagnosis, survival subtyping, and drug-sensitivity prediction from gut and intra-tumor microbiome profiles across a broad spectrum of cancer types.

Microbial Community Dynamics

Temporal-aware machine learning and digital-twin frameworks for personalized prediction of microbiome trajectories and human life-stage tracing.

Functional Mining of Microbial Genomes

Context-aware protein language models for deciphering biosynthetic gene clusters and discovering antimicrobial peptides from extreme environments.
