About

I am a direct-track Ph.D. student in bioinformatics and computational biology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), working in Prof. Kang Ning's group. My research focuses on developing deep learning and large-scale pretrained foundation models for microbiome analysis — including microbial community dynamics, biosynthetic gene cluster mining, and antimicrobial peptide discovery.

Research Directions

Microbiome Foundation Models

Large-scale pretrained and generative language models (MGM) for interpretable, general-purpose microbiome data analysis across diverse contexts.

Microbial Community Dynamics

Temporal-aware machine learning and digital-twin frameworks for personalized prediction of microbiome trajectories and human life-stage tracing.

Functional Mining of Microbial Genomes

Context-aware protein language models for deciphering biosynthetic gene clusters and discovering antimicrobial peptides from extreme environments.

Transfer Learning for Microbiome Analysis

Learning and transferring knowledge across cohorts and regions for robust microbiome-based classification and prediction.
