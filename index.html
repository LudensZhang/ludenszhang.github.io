01About

Biology is complex. Understanding it should be possible.

I am a direct-track Ph.D. student in bioinformatics and computational biology at HUST, working in Prof. Kang Ning’s group.

My research brings deep learning and large-scale pretraining to microbiome analysis. I am interested in representations that transfer across communities, cohorts and biological contexts—and in making what these models learn interpretable.

My work spans microbiome foundation models, microbial community dynamics, biosynthetic gene clusters, antimicrobial peptide discovery and auditable AI agents for metagenomic analysis.

03Featured research

MGM2, version 2.

v2bioRxiv preprint · posted 30 September 2026

MGM2 as a Multimodal Foundation Model for Microbial Community States and Responses

MGM2 represents a microbial community by combining DNA language-model embeddings of each microbe’s sequence with its abundance and a description of the sample. The revised preprint shows how this shared representation helps predict how communities respond to fecal microbiota transplantation, to drugs and during recovery, and uses sparse features to interpret what the model learns.
